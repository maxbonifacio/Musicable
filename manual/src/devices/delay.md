# Delay

Echoes, in time with the song or in milliseconds. With **Sync** on, the
echoes are a **Note** apart at the song's tempo, and with it off, the
**Time** apart. A new time fades in as the echoes at the old one fade out,
so they never chirp or click.

Each echo comes back at the **Feedback** level of the one before, through
the **Low Cut** and the **High Cut**, so the repeats thin out as they go.
**Ping Pong** sends each echo to the other side. Echoes saturate softly
above full scale, so a feedback near 100% can't run away. **Mix** blends
the echoes with the dry signal, which stays at full level up to halfway and
then fades.
