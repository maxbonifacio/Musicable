# Reverb

A room, a hall or a cave behind the signal. The signal goes through the
**Low Cut** and the **Pre-Delay** into the reverb, whose tail falls by 60 dB
over the **Decay** time, whatever its **Size**, and faster above the
**Damping** frequency. A new size glides into place, so it never clicks.

**Width** spreads the tail from mono to full stereo, and **Mix** blends it
with the dry signal, as the delay's does.
