# EQ

An eight-band EQ, then an output gain. Each band that's on is a cut, a
shelf, a bell or a notch at its frequency, and a band that's off lets
everything through.

A bell's or a shelf's **Gain** is in decibels, and its **Q** sets how wide
it is, or how steep for a shelf. The cuts take away 12, 24 or 48 dB per
octave past their frequency, and a higher Q raises a resonant peak at it.
